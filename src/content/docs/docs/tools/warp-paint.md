---
title: Warp Paint
description: Paint organic distortions onto your art to follow curved or uneven surfaces.
---

![The Warp Paint tool](/img/mac-slide4.png)

Warp Paint bends the picture wherever you paint. It's for the surfaces four corners can't describe: curves, folds, bumpy walls, objects.

:::note
Warp Paint is **shared by every grid** in the project. Painting once corrects them all, so they stay perfectly lined up.
:::

## Painting a warp

1. Switch **Enable** on. When it's off, the tool opens but nothing is painted, and the app shows "Warp Paint is off. Turn it on to draw."
2. Choose a **DIRECTION** with the four arrows. The chosen arrow lights up. This is the way the picture is pushed. Tap it again to deselect.
3. Set the **Brush Size** and **Brush Strength**.
4. Drag over the picture. Wherever you paint, the art is pushed in the chosen direction. Paint more in the same place to push further.

A circle shows the size of the brush.

## Seeing what you're doing

**Display Grid Overlay** draws grid lines over every grid while you paint, so you can see exactly how the picture is being bent. It's off by default and also switches off when you hide the controls.

## Fixing mistakes

- **Undo arrow:** undoes the last stroke.
- **Trash can:** clears *all* the warp paint. You can undo it.

## Turning the warp on and off

**Enable** turns the saved warp on or off for all grids without deleting it. Warp Paint is saved with the project.
