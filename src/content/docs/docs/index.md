ILW Light Mapper turns your device into a projection mapping studio. You pick the art, fit it exactly onto a wall, window, sculpture or any other surface, shape it with masks and warps, and send it to a projector.

![Pick the art, line it up, light up the room](/img/mac-slide2.png)

## What you can do

- **Fit video to any surface** by dragging four corner pins, with as many layers ("grids") as you like.
- **Mask** parts of the picture with additive and subtractive shapes.
- **Warp** the picture with a paintbrush to follow curved or uneven surfaces.
- **Tune** color, background key, audio and playback speed for each layer.
- **Use real-time shaders**, your own videos and images, or free online video.
- **Cast** to a projector or screen with AirPlay.
- **Save** every setup by name and move it between devices.

## Where to start

1. **[Your first projection](/docs/quick-start/)** takes about five minutes.
2. **[How it fits together](/docs/concepts/)** explains grids, projects and which controls affect what.
3. Browse **The screens** and **Tools** in the sidebar when you want to know exactly what a button does.
4. Keep **[Gestures and shortcuts](/docs/gestures/)** handy. Several features are hidden behind a long press.
