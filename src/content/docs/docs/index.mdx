ILW Light Mapper works in **two steps**. This whole guide is organized around them.

| | What you do | Where you end up |
|---|---|---|
| **Step 1: Align** | Choose your art, then drag its corners until it fits your surface exactly | Your art lines up with the wall, window or object |
| **Step 2: Refine** | Mask, warp, color and play it, then save | A finished projection you can save, reopen and cast |

You can go back to Step 1 from Step 2 at any time.

---

## Step 1: Align

Get your art onto the surface, in the right place.

1. On the **Main menu**, tap **NEW PROJECT**.
2. **Choose your art.** Tap a thumbnail on the **VIDEOS** tab, then **Download** if needed, then **SELECT**. You can also use **MY IMPORTS**, **SHADERS** and **ONLINE**. See [Choose artwork](/docs/step-1/artwork/).
3. **Fit it to your surface.** Four round pins appear at the corners of your art. Drag each pin to the matching corner of your wall, window or object.
4. Want more than one picture? Tap **+ GRID** to add another layer. Tap **CHANGE ART** to give it its own video.
5. When it fits, tap **SAVE + CONTINUE** to move on to Step 2.

<Screenshot name="calibration-full" alt="Step 1: dragging the corner pins to fit the art" fallback="/img/mac-slide2.png" />

:::tip
Tough corner to reach? Pick it in **Corner Selection** and move it a little at a time with the **NUDGE** arrows. Hold an arrow to keep going.
:::

More detail: [Align your grids](/docs/step-1/align/).

---

## Step 2: Refine

Now shape it and make it look the way you want.

1. Pick a tool from the **TOOLS** list on the right:
   - **[Mask](/docs/step-2/mask/):** show the art only inside shapes you choose.
   - **[Warp Paint](/docs/step-2/warp-paint/):** paint a warp so the art follows curves and bumps.
   - **[Color](/docs/step-2/color/):** change hue, saturation, brightness or tint.
   - **[Background Key](/docs/step-2/background-key/):** make a color, such as black, transparent.
   - **[Audio](/docs/step-2/audio/)** and **[Shader](/docs/step-2/shader/)** for sound and live visuals.
2. Use the **[playback bar](/docs/step-2/playback/)** along the bottom to play, pause and scrub.
3. Tap **HIDE CONTROLS** to see only your projection. Tap anywhere to bring the controls back.
4. Tap **SAVE PROJECT**.

<Screenshot name="final-display" alt="Step 2: the tools and the playback bar" fallback="/img/mac-slide3.png" />

More detail: [Step 2 overview](/docs/step-2/overview/).

:::caution[Save before going back to Step 1]
**GRID SETUP** takes you back to Step 1. If you have unsaved changes they are discarded, so tap **SAVE PROJECT** first.
:::
