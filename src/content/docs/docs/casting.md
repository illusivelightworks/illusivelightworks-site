---
title: Casting to a screen
description: Send your projection to an AirPlay device, and what Picture-in-Picture does.
---

You can send the finished projection to an AirPlay device such as an Apple TV or an AirPlay-capable projector, instead of plugging in a cable.

## Starting a cast

1. Tap the **Cast** button (corner calibration and Final Display both have one).
2. Choose your device from the list that appears.
3. While a device is receiving the cast the button is lit white. A short status line says what's happening.

To stop, tap **Cast** again. That stops all casting.

The Cast button only appears on devices that can cast.

## Picture-in-Picture

While you're casting, swiping out of the app to the Home Screen brings up a small **Picture-in-Picture** window. This keeps the app running so the projection keeps playing while you use something else.

## Quality

The **Cast quality** setting (Low, Medium, High) changes the bit rate of the stream. It takes effect the next time you start a cast. The picture size follows the **Screen Resolution** setting. See [Settings](/docs/settings/).

:::note
If another app starts playing its own video to the same AirPlay device, it can take over the screen. Stop the other app's AirPlay session, then cast again.
:::
