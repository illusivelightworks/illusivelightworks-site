---
title: Your first projection
description: Go from a blank project to art lined up on a wall in a few minutes.
---

You will need a projector (or a screen) and something to project onto. A plain wall is fine for a first try.

## 1. Start a new project

On the **Main menu**, tap **NEW**.

## 2. Choose some art

The artwork screen opens. Tap a thumbnail on the **VIDEOS** tab to see its details on the right. If it isn't on your device yet, tap **Download**, then **SELECT**.

You can also use the **MY IMPORTS**, **SHADERS** and **ONLINE** tabs. See [Choosing artwork](/docs/artwork/).

## 3. Line it up (Corner calibration)

Your art appears with four round pins at its corners.

1. **Drag each pin** to the matching corner of your surface. Or choose a corner with **Corner Selection** and move it a little at a time with the **NUDGE** arrows.
2. To move or resize the whole picture, drag it with one finger, or pinch and twist with two.
3. Need more art? Tap **+ GRID** to add another layer, and **CHANGE ART** to give it its own video.
4. When it fits, tap **SAVE + CONTINUE**.

More detail: [Corner calibration](/docs/calibration/).

## 4. Refine it (Final Display)

This is the main workspace. Pick a tool from the **TOOLS** list on the right:

- **Mask** to hide parts of the picture.
- **Warp Paint** to bend the picture to match curved surfaces.
- **Color** to change hue, saturation, brightness or add a color overlay.
- **Audio**, **Shader** and more.

The bar along the bottom plays, pauses and scrubs your video. See [Final Display](/docs/final-display/).

## 5. Save

Tap **SAVE PROJECT**. A "You have unsaved changes!" note appears under the button whenever there's something to save, and the button dims once everything is saved.

Next time, tap **LOAD LATEST** on the Main menu to pick up where you left off, or **SAVED SETUPS** to open any project by name. See [Saving and saved setups](/docs/projects/).
