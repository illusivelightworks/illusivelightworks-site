## My changes disappeared after tapping GRID SETUP

**GRID SETUP** returns to calibration on the *last saved* version of the project, so unsaved Final Display edits are discarded. Tap **SAVE PROJECT** first.

## A control does nothing, and I see "This control only works for Video grids" (or Shader grids)

You've selected a grid of the wrong kind. Video controls (timeline, play/pause, speed, audio) need a video grid. The Shader tool needs a shader grid. Pick a different grid with the arrows or numbered tiles.

## I can't select a grid, it's behind another

In corner calibration, turn off **Auto Grid Select** and use the numbered tiles. You can also change **Layer Order**.

## Warp Paint doesn't paint

Check that **Enable** is on and a **DIRECTION** is chosen. The app shows "Warp Paint is off. Turn it on to draw." when it's off.

## A grid shows "no media"

The video or image isn't on this device any more. This happens after importing a project that used your own media, or after removing a download. Tap **CHANGE ART** and choose the media again. Its alignment and settings are kept.

## An imported project is still downloading

Library and online videos download in the background after import, with progress messages. You can open the project meanwhile; grids show "no media" until their video arrives, and the video shows the next time you open the project. If a video can't be found (for example it was removed from its source), the app tells you how many couldn't be downloaded.

## It runs slowly or the app closes

- Lower **Screen resolution** in [Settings](/docs/settings/).
- Use fewer grids, or shorter and lower-resolution videos.
- Turn off effects you don't need.
- Remove downloads you're not using.
- Close other apps.

## Casting stops or shows another app's video

See [Casting to a screen](/docs/casting/).

## I need more help

Send a message from the **ABOUT** screen inside the app.
